## 📖 About The Project

This AI-powered assistant solves the major problem of drafting legal documents, which traditionally takes hours. With the help of JurisTech's autonomous AI engine, advocates can generate comprehensive, court-ready legal documents within minutes. Users can easily draft, edit, format, and share their drafted documents directly from the preview section of this assistant. JurisTech aims to solve major bottlenecks in the Indian legal industry.

🌐 **Live Application:** [juristech-official.vercel.app](https://juristech-official.vercel.app/)

---

## 🤖 Meet "Anvik" — The AI Engine
At the core of JurisTech is **Anvik**, an advanced autonomous AI legal engine designed specifically for Indian Jurisprudence. 
* **Autonomous Legal Logic:** Anvik doesn't just fill in blanks; it analyzes raw factual nuances, applies relevant sections of Indian laws (BNS, IPC, CPC, TPA, NI Act, etc.), and autonomously cites landmark Supreme Court and High Court judgments.
* **Court-Ready Formatting:** Generates complete drafts perfectly structured with proper jurisdiction headings, cause titles, statutory grounds, prayer clauses, and verifications.
* **Anti-Loop Mechanism:** Built with strict structural guardrails to prevent AI hallucination and repetitive looping, ensuring professional quality every single time.

---

## ✨ Key Features
* **13 Specialized Documents:** Pre-built tailored workflows for Legal Notices, Rent Agreements, Civil Suits, Police FIRs, Cheque Bounce (Sec 138 NI Act) complaints, and more.
* **Anvik:** A highly dynamic autonomous AI legal engine allowing users to generate comprehensive drafts for *any* unique case or legal scenario.
* **Granular Control:** Toggle between different document versions (from Short to Comprehensive) and adapt the writing style with Formal, Persuasive, or Professional tones.
* **One-Click Export:** Instantly download final drafts as **PDF, DOCX, or TXT** files.
* **Cloud Sync & Security:** Seamless user authentication, advocate profile management, and draft history powered by Supabase.

## 💻 Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
* **AI Engine:** Google Gemini API (gemini-2.5-flash)
* **Backend & Database:** Supabase
* **Document Processing:** jsPDF, docx
